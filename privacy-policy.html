Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10.

This Privacy Policy explains what information IPTV Guide Center (“we”, “us”, “this website”) collects when you visit, how it is used, and the choices available to you. We aim to collect as little information as possible.

Information we collect

This website does not require an account, registration, or subscription, and we do not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details. We do not include a contact form that stores data on this site.

Server logs

Like virtually all websites, our hosting provider may automatically keep standard server logs for security and reliability purposes. These logs may include your IP address, browser type and version, operating system, the referring page, and the date and time of your request. This information is used to diagnose technical issues, prevent abuse, and keep the site available. We do not use it to profile visitors or build advertising profiles.

Information you send us voluntarily

If you choose to contact us through the contact page, the information you include (such as your email address and the content of your message) is used only to respond to your inquiry. We do not add you to mailing lists or share it with third parties, except where necessary to deliver the reply or when required by law.

Cookies and analytics

This website, as delivered, sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics or advertising services. No tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or cross-site tracking technologies are used. Because we do not set non-essential cookies, we do not display a cookie consent banner. If we add analytics or advertising in the future, we will update this policy before doing so and, where required, obtain your consent.

Your browser may still store technical data locally (such as cached pages) for performance reasons; this is standard browser behavior and is under your control via your browser settings.

Third-party links and embedded content

Our articles contain links to other websites, including 8K IPTV 4U, as well as to software developers and device manufacturers. When you follow a link to another website, that site’s own privacy policy applies — we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any site you visit.

We do not embed third-party frames or widgets that would receive your IP address automatically. Any external resource on our own pages is limited to assets we host ourselves.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights and choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/EEA or the CCPA in California), you may have rights regarding personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete it. Because we do not maintain user databases or sell data, the practical scope of data we could act on is limited to voluntary correspondence. If you have sent us a message and wish to have it deleted, contact us and we will remove it.

Data retention and security

Server logs are retained only for a limited period as determined by our hosting provider. Voluntary correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your inquiry. We take reasonable technical measures to protect the site, but no method of transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the site or legal requirements. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the current version. Significant changes will be reflected on this page, and continued use of the site after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
